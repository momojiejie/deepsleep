DeepSleep

Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

DeepSleep is a bedtime stories app for parents and caregivers. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. In short: there are no accounts, no ads, no tracking across other apps, and we never sell your information.

Information kept on your device

These stay on your iPhone, in the app's own storage, and are not sent to us:

  • The child's first name, age range, bedtime and favorite themes you enter during setup.
  • Favorites, the stories you make, your edits, and the pictures painted for them.
  • Your settings, such as narrator, background music and volume.
  • Saved narration audio, so stories don't need to be downloaded again.

Deleting the app deletes all of this.

Information sent to service providers

Some features need an online service. We send only what each feature needs:

Writing and changing stories (Anthropic). When you make a story or ask for a change, your story idea, the child's first name and age range, the chosen art style and length, and (for changes) the story text are sent to Anthropic's Claude service to write the story.

Narration (MiniMax). When a story is read aloud by one of the app's narrators, the text of the page is sent to MiniMax to create the audio.

"Your voice" (MiniMax). If you choose to record your voice, the app records about 11 seconds of audio and sends it to MiniMax to create a voice model that reads stories in your voice. The recording and voice model are used only for this. Recording again deletes your previous voice model, and MiniMax automatically deletes a voice model that hasn't been used for 7 days. The microphone is used only while you are recording.

Painting pictures (Black Forest Labs). When you paint a story's pictures, a description of each page's scene and its characters, which may include names from the story, is sent to Black Forest Labs to create the images.

Subscriptions (Apple and RevenueCat). Purchases are handled by Apple. RevenueCat helps the app check whether your subscription is active, using an anonymous app identifier and your purchase records. We never see your payment details.

These providers process the information to deliver the feature, under their own privacy terms. Nothing is sent to them unless you use that feature.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics that follow you across apps or websites.
  • No selling or renting of personal information.

Children

DeepSleep is meant to be set up and used by a parent or caregiver, who reads or plays stories for their child. We don't ask children to enter information, and we don't knowingly collect personal information directly from children. A child's first name entered by a parent is used only to personalize stories, as described above. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices

  • You can use the story library without making stories, recording your voice or painting pictures.
  • You can turn off microphone access in iPhone Settings.
  • To ask about or delete information held by a provider, such as a voice model, email us and we'll take care of it.

Security

Information sent to providers travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Information on your device is protected by your iPhone's built-in security.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
