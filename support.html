How does the free trial work?

New members get 3 days free. You won't be charged if you cancel before the trial ends. After that, DeepSleep Pro renews monthly or yearly, depending on the plan you picked, until you cancel.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then DeepSleep, and choose Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

I already subscribed but the app asks me again

Tap "Restore purchases" at the bottom of the subscription screen while signed in to the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

How many stories can I make?

Each month, members can make 8 new stories, ask for up to 3 rewrites of each story, and paint the pictures for 3 stories. During the free trial it's 3 stories and 1 painted story. Listening to stories is unlimited. Allowances reset on the 1st of each month.

How does "Your voice" work?

Record about 10 seconds of yourself reading in a quiet room, and stories can be read in your voice. For best results, speak naturally and keep the phone about a hand's width from your mouth. If you record again, your previous voice is replaced. Your voice is removed automatically if it isn't used for 7 days. Just record again.

A story or picture didn't come out right

Open the story and tap "Edit story" to change any words, or describe a change and tap "Rewrite". Pictures for pages you change can be painted again.
